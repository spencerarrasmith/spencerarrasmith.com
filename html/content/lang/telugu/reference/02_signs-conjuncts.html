Signs & Conjuncts

Two charts. Vowel signs shows every consonant on its own and then with each sign, the way a Telugu chart is laid out. Conjunct forms shows each consonant in full and as the second letter of a cluster. Gold numbers mark combinations that are not drawn the usual way; the notes explain each one. Shapes are as drawn in Noto Sans Telugu, the font this app uses, and handwriting or other fonts differ in detail.
