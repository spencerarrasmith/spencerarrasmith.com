Bookish Telugu

Telugu writing keeps a few letters that modern Telugu no longer uses, and spells words taken from Sanskrit the Sanskrit way. None of this is practiced in the games or on the keyboard; it is here so that you can recognize it in older or more formal writing.

Letters Out of Use

Traditional alphabets still list these, and you may meet them in older books, grammars, and dictionaries. Modern spelling does without them.

Sanskrit Loanwords

Words borrowed from Sanskrit (tatsama, "same as that") keep their Sanskrit spelling, and with it the sounds native Telugu words rarely use: the aspirated consonants (ఖ ఘ ఛ ఝ ఠ ఢ థ ధ ఫ భ), ఋ, శ and ష, క్ష, జ్ఞ, and ః. In everyday speech many speakers say some of these more softly than they are written, an aspirate like the plain consonant, for example.
