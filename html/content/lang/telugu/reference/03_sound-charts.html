Vowels

Front of the mouth on the left, back on the right; tongue high at the top, low at the bottom. A filled dot is a Telugu vowel, a ring an English one.

Consonants

Lips on the left, throat on the right. Where a box holds a pair, the voiceless sound is on the left and the voiced one on the right. A gold box is a sound both languages have; an English sound in a plain box is one Telugu does not have.
