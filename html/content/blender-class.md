# Introductory Courses on Blender

## Coll 179 - Fall 2012 at Rice University
I taught an accredited (1 hour) course at Rice University during my junior year, meeting weekly for 1 hour over the course of a semester. 15 students learned Blender from the very basics, and I learned how to build a syllabus and handle a range of learning types.

## Blender 101 - Spring 2021 at Ball Aerospace
At Ball Aerospace, I was granted permission to teach a 4 x 1 hour, weekly lunch meeting to fellow coworkers. With an average attendance of 10, I was able to spark some interest in Blender as both a hobby tool and also showcase how it could be used for the benefit of the company.

## B.E.A.T. - September 2026
At a Meetup I regularly attend, I asked permission to teach a 1-2 hour workshop on how to use Blender for audio visualization. We covered the very basics of keyframing, geometry nodes, and Blender's Python API.
